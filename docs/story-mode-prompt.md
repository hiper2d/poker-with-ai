# Story mode — the momentum scale (original design prompt)

Let's consider our Lord of the Rings example. The default team split: 3 good, 3 evil characters.

The pit boss generates the story and multiple pivots: a minor shift to good and evil, a major shift to good or evil (4 pivots). I think I have the idea: we have 5 states on the scale from bad to good: very bad, bad, neutral, good, very good. When a bad character loses (money goes to zero), this triggers a shift towards the opposite team. After X messages in the chat, the pit boss decides who won this word exchange in the chat — who moved the story in the most interesting and logical way. The pit boss doesn't have to choose a winner; the default, usual outcome should be neutral. But if it's not, then 1/2 of the shift happens. Someone losing the game (going broke) moves the shift to the closest full value. Therefore, 2 wins in the chat can cause a full shift. Once some team gets the third shift in their direction, they win. Or, if all the players of some team lost their money — they lose. When the shift happens, the opposed (losing) team gets rewarded. I'm not yet sure how exactly. A reward is a cheat (one from the list we define). Some amount of accumulated rewards (2 or 3) can allow resurrecting someone from the team (a buy-in with some budget) or adding this budget to some other player.

So, the game master creating the story needs to create 5 game states in the context of this story (very bad, bad, neutral, good, very good). When the shift happens, the storyteller should create a story transition from the current to the target state.
